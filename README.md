# Aerospace Propulsion R&D Portfolio 

This repository presents selected engineering work from my role as Project Associate – I at Indian Institute of Engineering Science & Technology (IIEST) Shibpur.

The work focuses on aerospace propulsion and thermal-fluid R&D, including droplet-impact testing, thermal-pressure experiments, high-speed imaging diagnostics, CFD-supported analysis, and experimental validation.

## Technical Focus

- Aerospace propulsion and thermal-fluid systems
- Droplet-impact and surface-interaction testing
- Heated and non-heated surface experiments
- Atmospheric and elevated-pressure test conditions
- High-speed imaging diagnostics
- VOF-based CFD analysis using ANSYS Fluent/CFX
- Heat-transfer and wall-interaction interpretation
- Experimental validation and technical reporting

## Engineering Work Summary

### Thermal-Pressure Experimental Setup
Built and used a thermal-pressure setup to reproduce propulsion-relevant test environments for droplet-impact studies under atmospheric and elevated-pressure conditions.

### Droplet-Impact Testing
Conducted droplet-impact experiments to study spreading, deformation, breakup, evaporation response, and heated-wall interaction under controlled operating conditions.

### High-Speed Imaging and Data Extraction
Generated time-resolved droplet morphology data and converted visual observations into engineering outputs such as spreading ratio, deformation history, breakup tendency, and thermal-response trends.

### CFD-Supported Analysis
Developed VOF-based CFD models in ANSYS Fluent/CFX to simulate droplet impact, interface evolution, evaporation, heat transfer, and wall interaction. Simulation trends were compared with experimental observations.

### Engineering Documentation
Converted experimental and CFD findings into technical reports, engineering plots, presentations, and structured recommendations for next-stage aerospace propulsion R&D.
